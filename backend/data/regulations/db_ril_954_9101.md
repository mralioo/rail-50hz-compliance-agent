## 4.2 Cable installation
Cables interfacing with railway-owned 50 Hz energy systems shall maintain a
minimum bending radius of **150 mm** during installation and final routing.
The maximum permissible cable pulling force is **500 N** for standard NYY-J
low-voltage cables.

## 4.5 Documentation
Planning documents must cite active guidelines only. Citations of retired
guidelines (e.g., **Ril 954.0107**, superseded 2021 by Ril 954.9101)
constitute template drift and trigger rejection during EBA review.
